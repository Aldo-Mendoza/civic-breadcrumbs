1. Who we are and what this policy covers

brdcrmbs is an independent guidance and record-keeping tool for public-service processes. It is not a government service and cannot access a government file, make a government decision, or submit an application for you.

This policy applies to the brdcrmbs website and its journey, breadcrumb, sign-in, and AI-assisted features. “We,” “us,” and “brdcrmbs” refer to the operator of this service. The privacy lead is identified in the Contact us section.

3. Why we use information

We use information only for purposes a reasonable person would consider appropriate in the circumstances, including to:

  • create, display, update, and preserve your journey and breadcrumbs;
  • generate the goal confirmation, step-by-step guide, breadcrumb interpretation, recovery help, and handoff summary you request;
  • authenticate signed-in users, maintain guest sessions, and transfer eligible guest work to a signed-in account;
  • operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the reliability and accessibility of the service;
  • prevent abuse, enforce usage limits, and investigate errors or security incidents;
  • respond to questions, privacy requests, and complaints; and
  • meet legal obligations and protect users, the public, and the service.

When you voluntarily provide information or ask us to run an AI-assisted feature, you consent to the collection, use, and disclosure described at or before that interaction. If we want to use personal information for a materially new purpose, we will explain that purpose and obtain consent when required by law. You may withdraw consent for future processing, subject to reasonable notice and any legal or contractual limits.

We do not use journey content for advertising, sell or rent personal information, or disclose it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

4. AI-assisted features and Google Gemini

brdcrmbs uses the Google Gemini Developer API. When you use an AI-assisted feature, the service sends only the text and context needed for that task. Depending on the feature, this can include:

  • your goal title and description, plus curated public-service source context, to summarize your request and build a guide;
  • a breadcrumb note and limited journey context to suggest structured details; or
  • limited journey and breadcrumb context to produce a recovery or handoff summary.

AI output can be incomplete or wrong. Review it before relying on it and verify important information with the responsible official organization.

Google's treatment depends on the API service tier

Under Google's current Gemini API terms, content sent through unpaid services, and generated responses, may be used to provide, improve, and develop Google products and may be reviewed by human reviewers. For paid services, Google states that prompts and responses are not used to improve its products, although limited logging may be retained for safety, security, and abuse prevention. The deployment's billing tier can change, so you should assume AI inputs may be processed under the unpaid-service terms unless the operator confirms otherwise.

Do not submit sensitive, confidential, or identifying information to an AI feature. Read the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service for Google's current terms.

AI results may be held in the application's in-memory cache for up to 15 minutes to reduce duplicate requests. Goal-preview output is not otherwise saved as a separate record. A breadcrumb interpretation is saved only after you confirm it; generated guides and guide steps are saved as part of the journey.

7. How long we keep information

  • Guest data: guest sessions expire after 7 days. Associated guest journeys become eligible for deletion after expiry. Cleanup is triggered by later service activity, so deletion may not be immediate.
  • Signed-in account and journey data: retained while the account is used and until you request deletion, or until it is no longer reasonably needed for the purposes in this policy, subject to legal requirements.
  • AI cache: eligible AI results are cached in application memory for up to 15 minutes.
  • Operational information: security logs, error logs, communications, and rate-limit counters are kept only as long as reasonably needed for security, support, abuse prevention, legal compliance, and reliable operation.

Deletion from active systems may not immediately remove residual copies in provider backups or security logs. Providers may keep information under their own documented retention periods and legal obligations. We may also retain information when law requires it or when reasonably necessary to establish, exercise, or defend a legal claim.

8. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information, including encrypted network connections, secure production cookie settings, access controls, managed authentication, rate limiting, and limiting application logs that intentionally include journey content. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

If you believe your information or account may have been compromised, contact the privacy lead promptly.

9. Processing outside Canada

Service providers, including Render, Auth0/Okta, and Google, may process or store information in the United States and other jurisdictions outside Canada. Information in another jurisdiction may be available to courts, law-enforcement agencies, and national-security authorities under that jurisdiction's laws.

10. Your privacy choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to:

  • explain whether we hold personal information about you and how it has been used or disclosed;
  • provide access to that information;
  • correct inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • delete information that is no longer required, subject to lawful exceptions;
  • withdraw consent to future collection, use, or disclosure where consent is the basis, understanding that some features may then be unavailable; or
  • answer a question or investigate a privacy complaint.

You can edit a goal and individual breadcrumbs in the app, and delete individual breadcrumbs. For account access, full journey deletion, or another privacy request, email the privacy lead. We may need to verify your identity before responding and will never ask for your password. We will respond within the time required by applicable law or explain any lawful refusal.

If you use brdcrmbs for or with a minor, provide only information necessary for the task and ensure you have authority to provide it.

11. Changes to this policy
